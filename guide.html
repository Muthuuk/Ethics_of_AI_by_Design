How a chapter works

  1. Start. Answer three quick questions and choose how much time you have. This sets your path: Step by step or Fast track.
  2. Watch. Each lesson is a short 3D animation narrated by Sam. Press Play. The scene changes as Sam talks, and you can drag it to turn it. Prefer reading? Choose Read and tap a paragraph to see its part of the scene.
  3. Try it. Some lessons have a sorting activity: tap an item, then tap the box it belongs in.
  4. Check. Answer one question. Say first whether you are sure. If you miss it, you get a plain-words summary and a second chance.
  5. Case. Decide what to do in a short real-world scenario. Each choice gets feedback.
  6. Final test. When you finish the lessons, take the chapter's final test.

Your learning index

Each chapter gives you a score out of 100, worked out from four parts: knowledge (35%), applying it in cases (35%), self-awareness, meaning how often your confidence matched your answer (15%), and completion (15%). Reach 60 or more after the final test to download a certificate.

Good to know

Where is my progress saved?
In this browser only. Nothing is sent anywhere. Use the same browser and device to keep your record.
I can't hear Sam.
Voices come from your device. Pick a voice in the lesson controls, or choose "No voice: captions only".
The 3D scene does not show.
Some older devices cannot show 3D. The narration and captions carry the full lesson, so you will not miss anything.
Motion makes me uncomfortable.
Turn on "reduce motion" in your device settings. The scenes will then stay still apart from the changes that explain each point.
Labs
Three hands-on labs (loan bias, station risk spotter, checklist and audit builder) are linked from the home page and from the lessons they belong to.
